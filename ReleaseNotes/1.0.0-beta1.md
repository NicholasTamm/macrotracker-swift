# Release notes — 1.0.0 (Beta 1)

First TestFlight build of the MacroFactor clone. Everything is unlocked and
free — no account, no subscription, no ads.

## What's in this build

- **Food Log tab**: timeline logging with a collapsing calendar week banner
  (Show / Show & Pin / Hide), food search (recents first, Open Food Facts
  backup), serving editor with metric/imperial units, meal assignment,
  copy/paste for foods, time blocks, and whole days, custom foods, recipe
  builder, food library, quick-add, and the "Your Plate" multi-add sheet.
- **Capture**: barcode scanning, nutrition-label OCR, photo logging (demo
  estimator — clearly marked), voice logging, recipe import from URL or
  cookbook photo. Reachable from the center (+) button.
- **Dashboard tab**: today's macro ring, expenditure and weight-trend cards,
  weekly calories, micronutrient coverage, and insight cards.
- **Strategy tab**: tracking hub — weigh-ins with trend chart, body
  measurements, progress photos with side-by-side compare, habit streaks,
  period tracking, step history.
- **Coaching engine**: adaptive expenditure estimation, weight-trend
  smoothing, macro planner, and weekly check-ins (Coached / Collaborative /
  Manual programs; bulk / cut / maintenance goals).
- **Health app sync**: 30-day weight + step import, background delivery,
  weigh-in write-back. Manual entries always win over synced values.
- **Reminders**: local notifications for meals, weigh-ins, and the weekly
  check-in, all deep-linking into the app. Widgets and Apple Watch support
  are wired (see known issues).

## Privacy

No account. All data stays on your device. No ads, no analytics SDK, no
data sold or shared. Health data never leaves the device.

## Known issues / open questions (Beta 1)

1. **Weight-trend day bucketing.** The trend chart buckets days in UTC while
   the rest of the app uses the device calendar, so a late-night weigh-in
   can appear under a different day in the trend chart vs. the history list.
   Open user decision — please weigh in (pun intended) on which is correct.
2. **Watch app unverified.** The watchOS target and WidgetKit extension are
   wired but cannot be verified until the first Xcode build on a Mac; if the
   watch target misbehaves it will ship in v2 with zero impact on the phone
   app.
3. **Photo logging is a demo.** The estimator is a clearly-marked stub; the
   real backend is a later milestone.
4. **Voice quantities are digits-only** ("two eggs" won't parse as 2).
5. **Capture-method logging doesn't feed the food streak yet** — only the
   Food Log tab and quick-add do. Widgets still update.
6. First Xcode build is the real verification for all Swift code in this
   build (written to review standard on Linux).
